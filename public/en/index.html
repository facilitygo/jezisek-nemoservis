How it works

Every family member has their own account.

Ježíšek invites

A parent (Ježíšek) invites family members by e-mail, or creates an account for a child without a phone.

Write the letter

Wishes, why you deserve them, shop links, photos and sizes. Then an angel carries the sealed letter to the star.

Solve, read, reserve

Ježíšek solves a 12-piece jigsaw to open the letter, then reserves the gifts they will get. Other Ježíšeks can see it.

🔒

Private by design

Letters are visible only to the author and the Ježíšeks they accepted. No tracking, no analytics, no ads.
