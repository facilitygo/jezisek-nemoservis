Getting started

First the Ježíšek (parent) signs up, then invites the children. The app is in Czech; button names are shown in Czech with a translation.

For the Ježíšek (parent, grandparent…)

1. Create a Ježíšek account

Install the app – currently a beta via TestFlight (open the link on your iPhone), soon on the App Store. Open it, switch to Registrace (Sign up), choose Jsem Ježíšek (I am Ježíšek), enter a name, e-mail and password (8+ characters) and tap Vytvořit účet (Create account).

2. Invite the children

In Moji hodní (My good ones) tap Spravovat (Manage). Then either:

  • Pozvat hodného (Invite) – enter the child's e-mail and tap Poslat pozvánku (Send invitation). The child signs up as Jsem hodný (I am good) with the same e-mail and taps Přijmout (Accept) on the golden invitation.
  • Založit účet dítěti (Create a child account) – for a child without a phone. Enter the child's name, an e-mail (used only for sign-in) and a password. The account is saved on your phone; switching back to your account requires Face ID or the device passcode.

3. Read letters and reserve gifts

Solve the 12-piece jigsaw to open a new letter, then tap Zamluvit – obstarám já (Reserve – I'll get it) under a wish so other Ježíšeks don't buy the same gift.

For the children

Sign up as Jsem hodný with the e-mail the invitation was sent to, accept the invitation, tap Napsat nový dopis (Write a new letter), add wishes and tap Odeslat Ježíškovi (Send to Ježíšek).
